What this does and does not model

  • Line compounds only. Each oxide is present or absent at unit activity. Real reduction runs through a homologous series with mutual solubility, and at the shallow end through oxygen-deficient rutile TiO2-x, which no set of line compounds can represent. That is why the answer moves as you deepen the series — try the depth control.
  • No carbon deposition. Graphite is not a species, so under CH4 all carbon leaves as CO. The gas comes out at H2:CO = 2:1 exactly, which is what happens when carbon has nowhere else to go. Treat those numbers as provisional.
  • Sealed vessel. The inert case assumes a closed headspace. Flowing gas sweeps the released O2 away and is an open-system question this does not answer.
  • Equilibrium only. Nothing here says how fast any of it happens.
  • No experimental validation. Every check in this work is internal consistency — two independent routes agreeing, and coexisting phases landing on their computed boundary. Nothing has been compared against a measured phase boundary.
