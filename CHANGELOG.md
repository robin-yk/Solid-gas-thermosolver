# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Added the isolated-surface-vacancy population model (`solidgas/vacancy_population.py`), the supplement's account of why the CO rate peaks after 600 C while the measured inventory keeps rising. Table S1 reproduces at the reported parameters and a cold-start fit recovers them.
- Reduced the system before integrating it. Writing q = n_iso + n_assoc the loss term cancels out of the sum, so q and n_below are closed form and one scalar equation is left. The closure n_iso + n_assoc + n_below = n_V,tot is then an identity rather than a check, and the scalar equation goes to an implicit solver, which is what makes the parameter search safe: an explicit step is unstable wherever a small barrier meets a large prefactor.
- Report the fit under all three residual definitions, because the measured rates span 86-fold and the supplement does not record which it minimised. The log and relative residuals both return the reported parameters; raw least squares is dominated by one sample and lands two decades away in the prefactor. Every quantitative claim in the supplement reproduces under the log residual, including the 3.76 eV / 0.94 probe at an attempt frequency and the correlation of 0.997.
- One sentence does not reproduce under any residual: the annealed sample is 33 percent below the active region, not most of it, so "in every sample" should read "in every reduced sample".
- Added a third workspace for it, with the residual definition as a control, and a parity gate holding `web/population.js` to the Python module across the parameter grid.
- Put the live solver back on the page. Removing the browser engine had removed the tool: you could no longer type a gas composition, a pressure, a vessel volume, an oxide mass or a starting phase and get an answer, which is what the equilibrium workspace was for. `web/activeset.js` is restored with its parity gate, and the surface workspace is now live too through a new `web/vacancy.js`, so a measured BET area can be entered instead of a diameter.
- Both browser engines are mirrors under a gate, and a test refuses a `web/*.js` engine that has no gate paired with it. `activeset.js` holds to 4 ulp, the difference between glibc's exp and V8's; `vacancy.js` holds bit for bit, because the bound has no exponential in it and the multiplication chains are now grouped the same way on both sides.
- The values the manuscript quotes are still read from the committed JSON rather than recomputed in the page, so what a reader types and what the paper says cannot drift.
- Cut the repository to the two calculations the manuscript uses. Gas-solid equilibrium stays; the surface-capacity comparison is now `solidgas/vacancy_inventory.py`, four lines of lattice geometry that return a bound rather than a partition. The three-class defect stat mech, the surface Monte Carlo, the radial particle profile, the transport clock, the space-charge check, the coarse-grained KMC, the steady-state redox model, the cycling model and the oxygen-potential dual are all removed, with their browser mirrors, oracles, plate pipeline and tests.
- Verified before deleting: across the whole reported reduction series, 30 to 190 umol-O/g, the 903-line Monte Carlo partition and the geometric bound agree to 0.0000 percentage points. The surface term the model returned was the reconstruction ceiling itself, a constant, at every inventory the paper measures.
- Retired the browser solver. The page displays the JSON that `scripts/reproduce_paper.py` writes and computes nothing, so there is one implementation of each calculation instead of two and the Python-to-JavaScript parity apparatus is gone. The verification standard is now two independent implementations, the engine and the 80-digit oracle.
- Added `scripts/reproduce_paper.py`, which writes every computed number in the manuscript to committed CSV, and a gate that fails if regenerating changes a byte. Added `analysis/dielectric_correlation.py`, which carries the published regression verbatim and can refit it once the four measured points are digitised.
- Rebuilt the site as two workspaces, gas-solid equilibrium and inventory against surface capacity, with the scope limit stated on the page: the below-surface inventory is not assigned among subsurface point defects, bulk defects or extended defects.

- Removed the legacy solvers and the two browser pages that ran them. The reaction-extent SLSQP minimiser, the species-space one it grew from, the first-generation oxide tool and the oxygen-potential dashboard are gone, with their build scripts, browser sources, frozen data and tests: 28 files and 8,600 lines. What survived of `equilibrium.py` is the species, phase and element tables it carried, now `solidgas/species.py`, which the active-set and oxygen-potential routes both read. One minimiser remains in the repository and a gate refuses a second.
